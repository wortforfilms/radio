Executive summary

Controlled public knowledge audio pilot

Radio Vaigyaaniq proposes a 90-day controlled pilot with Prasar Bharati for science, public knowledge, Indian civilizational literacy, regional music discovery, education, and youth-oriented audio storytelling.

This is not a broadcast-ready claim. Rights closure, playback proof, editorial review, release review, and commercial licensing remain blocked until supported by written evidence and human approval.

Education and science

Aligned to public-interest programming themes including education, literacy, science and technology, rural development, and broadcast innovation.
